# Chapter 01 — Foundation

---

# 1. Purpose

## Purpose

Establish the canonical asset philosophy, asset boundaries, and universal asset knowledge governing every Canonical Asset defined within the Coz We Care Asset Bible (CAB).

The Foundation establishes the minimum canonical knowledge required before any domain-specific Asset Specification Record (ASR) is defined.

---

## Canonical Decision

The Coz We Care Asset Bible (CAB) is the Single Source of Truth for all Canonical Asset Knowledge used throughout the Coz We Care Production System.

The Foundation defines universal asset knowledge only.

Implementation belongs exclusively to downstream Asset Specification Records.

---

## Rules

### FND-001

Every Canonical Asset shall comply with the Foundation.

### FND-002

The Foundation takes precedence over every downstream Asset Specification Record unless superseded by a future Canonical Decision.

### FND-003

Only universal asset knowledge belongs in the Foundation.

---

## Validation Checklist

- Foundation reviewed.
- Asset objective defined.
- Universal asset knowledge identified.

# 5. Asset Knowledge Model

## Purpose

Define the canonical knowledge structure used throughout the Asset Bible.

---

## Canonical Decision

The Asset Bible is composed of Asset Specification Records (ASRs).

Each ASR represents one Canonical Asset Knowledge Model.

Every Canonical Asset shall be specified by exactly one ASR.

---

## Rules

### AKM-001

Every ASR shall have one Canonical Purpose.

### AKM-002

Every ASR shall have one Canonical Owner.

### AKM-003

Every ASR shall have one Canonical Location.

### AKM-004

Every ASR shall be reusable.

### AKM-005

Every ASR shall be independently maintainable.

### AKM-006

Every ASR shall be uniquely referenceable.

---

## Validation Checklist

# 6. Asset Boundaries

## Purpose

Define the scope of the Asset Bible.

---

## Canonical Decision

The Asset Bible governs Canonical Asset Knowledge exclusively.

Governance, production workflow, operational procedures, publication workflow, implementation, and project management remain outside the scope of the Asset Bible.

Such knowledge shall be governed by the Coz We Care Production Bible (CPB) or other authoritative documents.

---

## Rules

### AB-001

Only Canonical Asset Knowledge belongs in the Asset Bible.

### AB-002

Implementation shall never be embedded within Asset Specification Records.

### AB-003

Operational knowledge shall remain external.

### AB-004

External standards shall be referenced rather than duplicated.

---

## Validation Checklist

- Asset scope confirmed.
- No implementation knowledge included.
- Boundary integrity preserved.
