# 001. Vision & Philosophy

## Purpose

Define the philosophical foundation of the Universal Production Bible (UPB).

This chapter establishes why the UPB exists, what it is intended to achieve, and the fundamental principles that govern every production specification.

---

## Definition

### DEFINITION-UPB001-001

The Universal Production Bible (UPB) is the canonical specification of universal production knowledge.

---

## Vision

### DEFINITION-UPB001-002

To establish a shared foundation of universal production knowledge that enables consistent, reusable, scalable, and continuously improving production across all projects.

---

## Mission

### DEFINITION-UPB001-003

To define, preserve, organise, and evolve universal production knowledge independently of projects, media, technologies, and implementation methods.

---

## Philosophical Principles

### RULE-UPB001-001

Universal production knowledge shall exist independently of any individual project.

---

### RULE-UPB001-002

Universal production knowledge shall be defined once and reused wherever applicable.

---

### RULE-UPB001-003

Canonical knowledge shall have exactly one authoritative source.

---

### RULE-UPB001-004

Project-specific knowledge shall extend universal knowledge without redefining it.

---

### RULE-UPB001-005

Universal knowledge shall remain independent of implementation.

---

### RULE-UPB001-006

Canonical knowledge shall evolve through validated production experience.

---

### RULE-UPB001-007

Every improvement to universal knowledge shall strengthen future production.

---

## Constraints

### CONSTRAINT-UPB001-001

The Universal Production Bible shall contain only universal production knowledge.

---

### CONSTRAINT-UPB001-002

The Universal Production Bible shall not contain project-specific implementation.

---

### CONSTRAINT-UPB001-003

The Universal Production Bible shall not duplicate canonical knowledge maintained elsewhere.

---

## Output

### OUTPUT-UPB001-001

A canonical philosophical foundation for every Production Bible.

---

## Canonical Statement

The Universal Production Bible defines the philosophy of universal production knowledge. Every Production Bible inherits this foundation while remaining responsible for project-specific implementation.

# 002. Universal Production Lifecycle

## Purpose

Define the canonical production lifecycle that governs every production activity performed under the Universal Production Bible.

This lifecycle is universal and shall be inherited by every Project Production Bible.

---

## Definition

### DEFINITION-UPB002-001

The Universal Production Lifecycle (UPL) is the canonical model that transforms knowledge into production outputs while continuously improving that knowledge through validated production experience.

---

## Core Principle

### RULE-UPB002-001

Production is a continuous cycle of knowledge transformation.

### RULE-UPB002-002

Every production activity shall consume knowledge.

### RULE-UPB002-003

Every production activity shall generate knowledge.

### RULE-UPB002-004

Validated knowledge shall improve future production.

---

## Lifecycle Model

### DEFINITION-UPB002-002

The Universal Production Lifecycle consists of four canonical phases.

### PHASE-UPB002-001

Acquire

Acquire all knowledge required for production.

### PHASE-UPB002-002

Transform

Transform validated knowledge into production outputs.

### PHASE-UPB002-003

Deliver

Deliver approved production outputs to their intended audience.

### PHASE-UPB002-004

Improve

Transform validated production experience into reusable canonical knowledge.

---

## Lifecycle Relationship

### RULE-UPB002-005

Each phase shall produce the inputs required by the next phase.

### RULE-UPB002-006

The Improve phase shall become the knowledge source for the next Acquire phase.

### RULE-UPB002-007

The lifecycle shall operate continuously.

---

## Behaviours

### BEHAVIOUR-UPB002-001

AI shall identify the current lifecycle phase before performing production activities.

### BEHAVIOUR-UPB002-002

AI shall execute activities appropriate to the active lifecycle phase.

### BEHAVIOUR-UPB002-003

AI shall preserve lifecycle continuity.

### BEHAVIOUR-UPB002-004

AI shall integrate validated learning into future production.

---

## Constraints

### CONSTRAINT-UPB002-001

Project Production Bibles shall inherit the Universal Production Lifecycle.

### CONSTRAINT-UPB002-002

Project-specific workflows shall implement the lifecycle without contradicting it.

### CONSTRAINT-UPB002-003

The canonical lifecycle shall remain independent of media, project type, implementation method and production technology.

---

## Output

### OUTPUT-UPB002-001

A universal production lifecycle applicable to every production system governed by the Universal Production Bible.

---

## Canonical Statement

The Universal Production Lifecycle defines the canonical production model of the Universal Production Bible. Every project-specific workflow is an implementation of this lifecycle. Every production cycle strengthens the next through validated knowledge.
