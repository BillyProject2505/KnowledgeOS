# Chapter 01 — Foundation

## 1. Purpose

### Purpose

Establish the universal production philosophy, production boundaries, and foundational rules that govern every Communication Artefact produced under the Coz We Care Production Bible (CPB).

The Foundation provides the minimum production knowledge required before any domain-specific production standards are applied.

### Canonical Decision

The Coz We Care Production Bible (CPB) is the canonical Production Knowledge Base for all Communication Artefacts produced under Coz We Care.

The Foundation defines universal production knowledge only.

Implementation knowledge belongs exclusively to its respective production chapter.

### Rules

#### FND-001

Every production decision shall begin with the Foundation.

#### FND-002

The Foundation shall take precedence over all subsequent chapters unless superseded by a future canonical decision.

#### FND-003

The Foundation shall define universal production knowledge only.

### Examples

#### ✔ Correct

```text
Foundation
↓

Brand System
↓

Audience System
↓

Content Strategy
↓

Content Architecture
↓

Editorial System
↓

Visual Design System
```

#### ✘ Incorrect

```text
Visual Design
↓

Brand System
↓

Audience
```

### Checklist

- Production objective identified.
- Foundation reviewed.
- Correct production sequence established.
- Domain chapter selected.

## 5. Production Knowledge Model

### Purpose

Define the canonical Production Knowledge Model used throughout the Production Bible.

### Canonical Decision

The Production Bible is a structured Production Knowledge Base composed of Production Knowledge Objects (PKOs).

### Rules

#### PKM-000

A Production Knowledge Object (PKO):

- has one canonical purpose;
- has one canonical owner;
- has one canonical location;
- is reusable;
- is independently maintainable;
- is referenceable.

#### PKM-001

Only production knowledge shall be included.

#### PKM-002

Every PKO shall be reusable.

#### PKM-003

Production knowledge shall be deterministic.

#### PKM-004

Production knowledge shall be modular.

#### PKM-005

Knowledge shall compose progressively.

#### PKM-006

Every concept shall have one canonical source.

### Checklist

- Production value confirmed.
- PKO identified.
- Canonical owner defined.
- Reusable.
- Non-duplicated.
