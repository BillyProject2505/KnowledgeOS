# Chapter 01 — Foundation

---

# 1. Purpose

## Purpose

Establish the canonical production philosophy, production boundaries, and universal knowledge that govern every Communication Artefact produced under the Coz We Care Production Bible (CPB).

The Foundation defines the minimum canonical production knowledge required before any domain-specific production knowledge is applied.

---

## Canonical Decision

The Coz We Care Production Bible (CPB) is the canonical Production Knowledge Base for every Communication Artefact produced under Coz We Care.

The Foundation defines universal production knowledge only.

Implementation belongs exclusively to downstream production chapters.

---

## Rules

### FND-001

Every production decision shall comply with the Foundation.

### FND-002

The Foundation shall take precedence over all downstream chapters unless superseded by a future Canonical Decision.

### FND-003

Only universal production knowledge belongs in the Foundation.

---

## Validation Checklist

- Foundation reviewed.
- Production objective defined.
- Universal knowledge identified.

---

# 3. AI-First Philosophy

## Purpose

Define how AI shall interpret and consume the Production Bible.

---

## Canonical Decision

The Production Bible is designed primarily for AI consumption.

Canonical knowledge shall optimize deterministic interpretation while preserving AI freedom within canonical constraints.

---

## Principles

### AIP-001

AI consumes canonical knowledge rather than implementation workflows.

### AIP-002

Canonical Decisions define constraints, not reasoning steps.

### AIP-003

AI may choose any reasoning path that preserves Canonical Decisions.

### AIP-004

Documentation shall remain concise, deterministic, modular, and machine-readable.

---

## Rules

### FND-007

Canonical Decisions shall precede all supporting knowledge.

### FND-008

Knowledge shall minimize ambiguity.

### FND-009

Documentation shall remain implementation-independent.

---

## Validation Checklist

- AI-first structure maintained.
- Canonical constraints preserved.
- No implementation workflow embedded.

# 5. Production Knowledge Model

## Purpose

Define the canonical structure of production knowledge.

---

## Canonical Decision

The Production Bible is composed of Production Knowledge Objects (PKOs).

Each PKO represents one canonical production concept.

---

## Rules

### PKM-001

Every PKO shall have one canonical purpose.

### PKM-002

Every PKO shall have one canonical owner.

### PKM-003

Every PKO shall have one canonical location.

### PKM-004

### PKM-005

Every PKO shall be independently maintainable.

Every PKO shall be referenceable.

## Validation Checklist
