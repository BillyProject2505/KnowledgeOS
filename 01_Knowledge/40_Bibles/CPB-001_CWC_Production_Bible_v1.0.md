# 001. Purpose & Scope

## Definition

### DEFINITION-CPB001-001

The CWC Production Bible is the canonical specification that governs the production of all official Coz We Care content.

## Principle

### PRINCIPLE-CPB001-001

Purpose-Driven Governance

The CWC Production Bible shall serve as the single authoritative specification for official Coz We Care content production.

## Scope

### SCOPE-CPB001-001

This document governs:

- Production principles
- Production specifications
- Production workflows
- Production quality requirements
- Production governance

### SCOPE-CPB001-002

This document does not govern:

- Reusable production assets
- Cross-project architectural principles
- Platform-specific optimization
- Platform-specific monetization
- Organization operations unrelated to official content production

## Structure

### STRUCTURE-CPB001-001

The CWC Production Bible is organized into the following canonical domains:

- Foundation
- Production Specifications
- Production Workflow
- Asset Integration

## Rule

### RULE-CPB001-001

All official Coz We Care content production shall conform to the canonical specifications defined within this document.

### RULE-CPB001-002

The CWC Production Bible shall serve as the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for all production specifications.

## Constraint

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-001

This document shall define production specifications only.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-002

Implementation specifications shall be defined only within their respective canonical chapters.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-003

This document shall not duplicate specifications governed by other canonical documents.

## Dependency

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-001

Universal Production Bible (UPB)

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-002

KnowledgeOS Specification Standard (KOS-ST)

## Output

### OUTPUT-CPB001-001

A complete, authoritative, and canonical production specification governing all official Coz We Care content production.

# 002. Core Principles

## Definition

### DEFINITION-CPB001-002

Core Principles define the fundamental principles that govern the interpretation, development, implementation, maintenance, and evolution of the CWC Production Bible.

## Principle

### PRINCIPLE-CPB001-002

Principle-Driven Specification

Every canonical production specification shall be derived from, interpreted through, and remain consistent with the Core Principles defined in this chapter.

## Scope

### SCOPE-CPB001-003

This chapter governs:

- Interpretation of production specifications
- Development of production specifications
- Revision of production specifications
- Validation of production specifications
- Evolution of production specifications

### SCOPE-CPB001-004

This chapter does not govern:

- Operational implementation
- Production workflows
- Platform-specific production requirements
- Reusable production assets
- Cross-project architectural principles

## Structure

### STRUCTURE-CPB001-002

The Core Principles are composed of the following canonical principles:

- AI-First
- Single Source of Truth (SSOT)
- Medical Accuracy
- Educational Integrity
- Non-Stigmatizing Communication
- Indonesian Context
- Identity Consistency
- Accessibility by Design
- Quality by Default
- Traceability

## Rule

### RULE-CPB001-004

Every canonical production specification shall conform to all applicable Core Principles.

### RULE-CPB001-005

No canonical production specification shall contradict any Core Principle.

### RULE-CPB001-006

Every revision of the CWC Production Bible shall preserve consistency with the Core Principles.

### RULE-CPB001-007

Core Principles shall govern the interpretation of all canonical production specifications when ambiguity exists.

## Constraint

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-004

This chapter shall define principles only.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-005

This chapter shall not define implementation specifications.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-006

Implementation requirements shall be defined only within their respective canonical chapters.

## Dependency

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-003

001 Purpose & Scope

## Output

### OUTPUT-CPB001-002

A consistent and principle-driven foundation governing the interpretation, development, implementation, maintenance, and evolution of every canonical production specification within the CWC Production Bible.

# 003. Brand Identity

## Definition

### DEFINITION-CPB001-003

Brand Identity defines the official identity of Coz We Care that shall be consistently preserved across all official content production.

## Principle

### PRINCIPLE-CPB001-003

Identity Consistency

The official Coz We Care Brand Identity shall remain consistent across all production outputs regardless of platform, format, or production workflow.

## Scope

### SCOPE-CPB001-005

This chapter governs:

- Official Brand Identity
- Identity domains
- Identity governance
- Identity consistency

### SCOPE-CPB001-006

This chapter does not govern:

- Visual implementation specifications
- Editorial implementation specifications
- Production workflows
- Reusable production assets
- Cross-project architectural principles

## Structure

### STRUCTURE-CPB001-003

The official Coz We Care Brand Identity consists of the following identity domains:

- Visual Identity
- Editorial Identity
- Communication Identity
- Educational Identity
- Brand Positioning

## Rule

### RULE-CPB001-008

Every Brand Identity domain shall be governed by its own canonical specification.

### RULE-CPB001-009

Every official Coz We Care production shall preserve all Brand Identity domains.

### RULE-CPB001-010

Brand Identity implementation shall be performed only through the canonical specifications referenced by this chapter.

### RULE-CPB001-011

No Brand Identity domain shall be modified except through the canonical governance process.

## Constraint

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-007

This chapter shall define Brand Identity only.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-008

This chapter shall not define implementation specifications.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-009

Implementation specifications shall be defined only within their respective canonical chapters.

## Dependency

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-004

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-005

Editorial Identity shall be implemented by:

- 007 Editorial Identity Standards

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-006

Communication Identity shall be implemented by:

- 007 Editorial Identity Standards

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-007

Educational Identity shall be implemented by:

- 007 Editorial Identity Standards

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-008

## Output

### OUTPUT-CPB001-003

Every official Coz We Care production shall consistently express a single, recognizable, and canonical Brand Identity across all supported production outputs.
