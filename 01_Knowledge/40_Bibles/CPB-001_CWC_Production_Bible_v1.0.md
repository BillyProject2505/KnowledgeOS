# 001. Purpose & Scope

## Definition

### DEFINITION-CPB001-001

The CWC Production Bible is the canonical specification that governs the production of all official Coz We Care content.

## Principle

### PRINCIPLE-CPB001-001

Purpose-Driven Governance

The CWC Production Bible shall serve as the single authoritative specification for official Coz We Care content production.

## Scope

### SCOPE-CPB001-001

This document governs:

- Production principles
- Production specifications
- Production workflows
- Production quality requirements
- Production governance

### SCOPE-CPB001-002

This document does not govern:

- Reusable production assets
- Cross-project architectural principles
- Platform-specific optimization
- Platform-specific monetization
- Organization operations unrelated to official content production

## Structure

### STRUCTURE-CPB001-001

The CWC Production Bible is organized into the following canonical domains:

- Foundation
- Production Specifications
- Production Workflow
- Asset Integration

## Rule

### RULE-CPB001-001

All official Coz We Care content production shall conform to the canonical specifications defined within this document.

### RULE-CPB001-002

The CWC Production Bible shall serve as the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for all production specifications.

## Constraint

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-001

This document shall define production specifications only.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-002

Implementation specifications shall be defined only within their respective canonical chapters.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-003

This document shall not duplicate specifications governed by other canonical documents.

## Dependency

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-001

Universal Production Bible (UPB)

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-002

KnowledgeOS Specification Standard (KOS-ST)

## Output

### OUTPUT-CPB001-001

A complete, authoritative, and canonical production specification governing all official Coz We Care content production.

# 004. Audience

## Definition

### DEFINITION-CPB001-004

The Audience Model defines the canonical classification of audiences served by official Coz We Care productions.

## Principle

### PRINCIPLE-CPB001-004

Audience-Centered Production

Every official production shall be planned and developed according to its intended audience.

## Scope

### SCOPE-CPB001-005

This chapter governs:

- Audience Model
- Audience classification
- Audience hierarchy
- Audience governance

### SCOPE-CPB001-006

This chapter does not govern:

- Audience implementation
- Editorial implementation
- Visual implementation
- Platform-specific targeting
- Marketing segmentation
- Advertising strategy

## Structure

### STRUCTURE-CPB001-004

The canonical Audience Model consists of the following audience groups:

- Primary Audience
- Secondary Audience
- Tertiary Audience

### STRUCTURE-CPB001-005

Primary Audience

The principal beneficiaries of official Coz We Care productions.

### STRUCTURE-CPB001-006

Secondary Audience

Individuals who directly support, influence, or accompany the Primary Audience.

### STRUCTURE-CPB001-007

Tertiary Audience

Individuals and organizations that influence the wider healthcare, education, policy, and community environment.

## Rule

### RULE-CPB001-012

Every official production shall identify its intended audience before production begins.

### RULE-CPB001-013

Audience selection shall guide production planning and execution.

### RULE-CPB001-014

A production may target one or more audience groups when appropriate.

### RULE-CPB001-015

Audience prioritization shall support production decisions without restricting multi-audience productions.

### RULE-CPB001-016

The Audience Model shall be applied consistently across all official Coz We Care productions.

## Constraint

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-010

This chapter shall define the Audience Model only.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-011

Audience implementation shall be defined only within their respective canonical chapters.

### CONSTRAINT-CPB001-012

Audience definitions shall remain independent of production platform.

## Dependency

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-009

005 Content Strategy

### DEPENDENCY-CPB001-010

007 Editorial Standards

## Output

### OUTPUT-CPB001-004

Every official Coz We Care production shall be planned using a consistent canonical Audience Model.
