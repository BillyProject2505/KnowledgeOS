>
> **Status:** Canonical (LOCK)

> **Type:** Core Specification
>
> **Parent Standard:** AI-First Documentation Standard (AIFDS)

---

# 1. Purpose

This specification defines the governance model of the AI-First Documentation Standard (AIFDS).

Its purpose is to ensure that specifications are governed through consistent, transparent, and traceable policies throughout their lifecycle.

---

# 2. Scope

This specification defines:

- Governance Principles
- Governance Roles
- Document Lifecycle
- Approval Policy
- Change Control Policy
- Retirement Policy
- Governance Records

This specification does not define implementation workflows or operational procedures.

---

# 3. Governance Principles

Governance shall be:

- transparent;
- traceable;
- consistent;
- repository-agnostic;
- implementation-independent;
- auditable.

Governance policies shall remain independent of implementation technology.

---

# 4. Governance Roles

The canonical governance roles are:

- Author
- Reviewer
- Approver
- Maintainer

Projects may define additional roles.

AIFDS defines responsibilities, not organizational assignments.

---

# 5. Document Lifecycle

Specifications shall progress through the following lifecycle:

1. Draft
2. Review
3. Approved
4. Canonical
5. Deprecated (optional)
6. Superseded (optional)
7. Archived (optional)

A specification shall occupy only one lifecycle state at a time.

---

# 6. Approval Policy

Before reaching Canonical status, a specification shall:

- complete Review;
- resolve identified issues;
- receive formal approval;
- record approval in the Change History.

Projects may introduce additional approval requirements.

---

# 7. Change Control Policy

Normative changes shall:

- identify the affected specification;
- identify the affected version;
- describe the rationale;
- comply with AIFDS-008 (Versioning).

---

# 8. Retirement Policy

A specification may be retired by:

- Deprecation
- Supersession
- Archival

Retired specifications shall remain identifiable and traceable.

Superseded specifications shall explicitly identify their replacement.

---

# 9. Governance Records

Governance activities should produce records such as:

- Approval Records
- Review Records
- Decision Records
- Change Records

The storage mechanism is implementation-specific.

---

# 10. Separation of Concerns

Governance defines policy.

Workflow defines process.

Operations define implementation.

These concerns shall remain independent.

---

# 11. Conformance

Documents claiming compliance with AIFDS shall comply with the governance requirements defined by this specification.
