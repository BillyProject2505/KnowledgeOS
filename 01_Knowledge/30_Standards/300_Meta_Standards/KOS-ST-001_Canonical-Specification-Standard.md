# KOS-ST-001 — Canonical Specification Standard

## Purpose

Define the mandatory specification standard for every canonical document within the Knowledge Operating System (KOS). This standard governs how canonical knowledge is structured, identified, written, referenced, maintained, and evolved.

This standard applies to every canonical document, including but not limited to:

- Principles
- Standards
- Frameworks
- Bibles
- Registries
- Templates
- References
- Decision Records
- Specifications
- Policies
- Procedures
- Guides
- Manuals

---

# Canonical Principles

## PRINCIPLE-KOSST001-001

Single Source of Truth

Every canonical specification shall have exactly one authoritative definition.

---

## PRINCIPLE-KOSST001-002

AI First

Specifications shall be written primarily for AI interpretation while remaining understandable to humans.

---

## PRINCIPLE-KOSST001-003

Production Driven

Canonical knowledge shall evolve from practical implementation and validated experience rather than theoretical completeness.

---

## PRINCIPLE-KOSST001-004

Minimal Narrative

Only information necessary to understand or operate the system shall be included.

---

## PRINCIPLE-KOSST001-005

Consistency

Equivalent information shall always use the same terminology, structure and formatting.

---

## PRINCIPLE-KOSST001-006

Atomic Specification

Every specification element shall contain exactly one canonical idea.

---

# Canonical Vocabulary

## VOCABULARY-KOSST001-001

The following specification element names are canonical.

- Definition
- Rule
- Behaviour
- Constraint
- Priority
- Dependency
- Output
- Exception

No alternative terminology shall be used for these specification element types.

---

# Canonical Identifier System

## RULE-KOSST001-001

Every canonical specification element shall use a permanent identifier.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-002

Identifiers shall remain stable throughout the lifecycle of the specification.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-003

Identifiers shall never be reused.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-004

Identifiers shall follow the hierarchical format:

TYPE-DOCUMENT-###

Examples:

RULE-UPB001-001

RULE-UCB001-002

BEHAVIOUR-KOS001-001

CONSTRAINT-ODS003-004

---

# Authoring Rules

## RULE-KOSST001-005

Each Definition shall define exactly one concept.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-006

Each Rule shall contain exactly one rule.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-007

Each Behaviour shall define exactly one behaviour.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-008

Each Constraint shall contain exactly one constraint.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-009

Each Priority shall contain exactly one priority.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-010

Each Dependency shall identify exactly one dependency.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-011

Each Output shall describe exactly one output.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-012

Each Exception shall describe exactly one exception.

---

# Governance

## RULE-KOSST001-013

This standard governs the structure of every canonical document within the Knowledge Operating System.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-014

Domain-specific standards may extend this standard but shall not contradict it.

---

## RULE-KOSST001-015

Changes to this standard shall only be made through canonical revision.
