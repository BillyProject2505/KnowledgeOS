This directory contains research and discovery artifacts supporting the Meta AI-Native Production System.

## Scope

Artifacts in this directory support evidence collection, research questions, discovery, analysis, and other pre-decision research activities.

Research artifacts do not automatically establish canonical architectural or governance authority.

Individual research artifacts remain authoritative only within their declared scope and stated status.
