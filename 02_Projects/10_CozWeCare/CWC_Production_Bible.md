## PB-FND-001 — Purpose

Status: LOCK
Version: 1.0
Type: Foundation

---

### Purpose

The CWC Production Bible defines the canonical production system for Coz We Care.

It serves as the Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for all production-related knowledge, standards, frameworks, specifications, policies, procedures, and governance.

The CWC Production Bible establishes a unified foundation that ensures every production output is consistent, reusable, scalable, maintainable, and aligned with the Coz We Care vision.

---

### Objectives

The CWC Production Bible SHALL:

- Define the canonical production architecture.
- Define production principles.
- Define production standards.
- Define production frameworks.
- Define production specifications.
- Define production governance.
- Define quality expectations.
- Define relationships between production domains.
- Enable consistent decision-making.
- Preserve institutional knowledge.
- Support effective collaboration between humans and AI.
- Enable sustainable long-term evolution of the production system.

---

### Intended Outcomes

The CWC Production Bible enables:

- Consistent production quality.
- Predictable production processes.
- Reusable production assets.
- Efficient onboarding.
- Easier maintenance.
- Clear governance.
- AI-first documentation.
- Continuous improvement.

---

### Canonical Statement

Every production decision SHOULD be traceable to the CWC Production Bible.

If no canonical guidance exists, the CWC Production Bible SHALL be updated through the approved governance process before establishing a new production standard.

---

### Success Criteria

The purpose of the CWC Production Bible is achieved when:

- Production standards are consistently applied.
- Production quality remains consistent across all outputs.
- Knowledge is preserved independently of individual contributors.
- Human and AI collaborators reference the same canonical source.
- Production evolves through governed change rather than ad hoc decisions.

---

### Relationships

Owns:

- Production System

Supports:

- Brand System
- Layout System
- Design System
- Content System
- Production Workflow
- Quality Assurance
- Governance & Maintenance

Referenced By:

- Entire CWC Production Bible

## PB-FND-002 — Scope

Status: LOCK
Version: 1.0
Type: Foundation

---

### Purpose

Define the governance boundary of the CWC Production Bible.

This section specifies what the Production Bible governs and what falls outside its authority.

---

### Scope Statement

The CWC Production Bible SHALL govern every aspect of content production for Coz We Care.

It provides the canonical standards that ensure production activities remain consistent, traceable, and maintainable.

---

### In Scope

The CWC Production Bible governs:

- Foundation
- Brand System
- Layout System
- Design System
- Content System
- Production Workflow
- Quality Assurance
- Governance & Maintenance

---

### Out of Scope

The CWC Production Bible SHALL NOT govern:

- Organizational management
- Financial management
- Human resource management
- Legal documentation
- Information technology infrastructure
- Software development
- Repository management

Unless explicitly incorporated through an approved canonical standard.

---

### Governance Boundary

The CWC Production Bible defines standards.

Operational execution SHALL be implemented through supporting documents such as:

- Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
- Work Instructions
- Project Documentation
- Campaign Plans
- Editorial Calendars
- Checklists

Supporting documents SHALL comply with the canonical standards defined by the CWC Production Bible.

---

### Canonical Principles

#### PB-FND-SCP-001

Title:

Clear Boundary

Statement:

Every governed domain SHALL have a clearly defined boundary.

---

#### PB-FND-SCP-002

Title:

Single Ownership

Statement:

Each responsibility SHALL have one canonical owner.

---

#### PB-FND-SCP-003

Title:

Standards Before Execution

Statement:

The Production Bible defines standards.

Supporting operational documents define implementation.

---

#### PB-FND-SCP-004

Title:

Canonical Authority

Statement:

When conflicts occur between supporting documentation and the CWC Production Bible, the CWC Production Bible SHALL take precedence unless superseded through the approved Change Management process.

---

### Success Criteria

This section is considered complete when:

- Governance boundaries are explicit.
- In-scope domains are identified.
- Out-of-scope domains are identified.
- Responsibilities do not overlap.
- Supporting documents clearly understand when to reference the Production Bible.

## PB-FND-005 — Terminology

Version: 1.0
Type: Foundation

---

### Purpose

Define the canonical terminology used throughout the CWC Production Bible.

This section establishes a shared vocabulary to ensure consistent interpretation across all production standards, workflows, and governance.

---

### Scope

The terminology defined in this section SHALL be used consistently throughout the CWC Production Bible.

A defined term SHALL retain the same meaning unless explicitly redefined by a more specific canonical standard.

---

### Canonical Terms

#### Production Bible

Definition:

The canonical document that defines the complete production system for Coz We Care.

---

#### Production System

Definition:

The integrated system consisting of standards, processes, governance, quality assurance, and supporting components required to produce consistent outputs.

---

#### Standard

Definition:

A mandatory rule that defines how production activities SHALL be performed.

---

#### Principle

Definition:

A fundamental rule that guides production decisions across the entire production system.

---

#### Framework

Definition:

A structured approach that organizes related concepts without prescribing every implementation detail.

---

#### Specification

Definition:

A detailed definition of requirements, characteristics, or constraints for a specific production component.

---

#### Workflow

Definition:

The ordered sequence of production activities performed to achieve a defined outcome.

---

#### Process

Definition:

A repeatable set of related activities within a workflow.

---

#### Procedure

Definition:

Documented instructions describing how a specific task is performed.

---

#### Asset

Definition:

Any reusable production resource used throughout the production lifecycle.

Examples include:

- Logos
- Templates
- Icons
- Illustrations
- Images
- Documents

---

#### Component

Definition:

A reusable building block within the production system.

---

#### Template

Definition:

A predefined structure intended for repeated use.

---

#### Pattern

Definition:

A reusable solution for solving recurring production problems.

---

#### Guideline

Definition:

A recommended practice that supports consistent implementation without being mandatory.

---

#### Policy

Definition:

A governing rule defining organizational expectations and constraints.

---

#### Governance

Definition:

The system responsible for ownership, decision-making, change management, and accountability.

---

#### Quality Assurance (QA)

Definition:

The systematic process used to verify compliance with canonical standards.

---

#### Canonical

Definition:

Officially approved as the authoritative reference.

Canonical content SHALL take precedence over non-canonical documentation.

---

#### Single Source of Truth (SSOT)

Definition:

The one authoritative source from which a specific type of knowledge is maintained.

---

#### AI Documentation (AI-DOC)

Definition:

Documentation primarily optimized for AI readability, parsing, retrieval, maintenance, and automation.

---

#### Human Documentation (H-DOC)

Definition:

Documentation primarily optimized for human learning, onboarding, explanation, and collaboration.

---

### Terminology Rules

#### PB-FND-TERM-001

Title:

Consistent Usage

Statement:

A canonical term SHALL be used consistently throughout the CWC Production Bible.

---

#### PB-FND-TERM-002

Title:

Single Definition

Statement:

Each canonical term SHALL have one authoritative definition.

---

#### PB-FND-TERM-003

Title:

No Synonym Substitution

Statement:

Canonical terms SHOULD NOT be replaced by informal synonyms when precision is required.

---

#### PB-FND-TERM-004

Title:

Future Expansion

Statement:

New canonical terms SHALL be added to this section before being used elsewhere in the Production Bible.

---

### Success Criteria

This section is considered complete when:

- Every key production concept has one canonical definition.
- Terminology is consistently applied throughout the Production Bible.
- Ambiguous terminology has been eliminated.
- Cross-domain communication uses the same vocabulary.

## PB-FND-010 — Foundation Governance

Status: LOCK
Version: 1.0
Type: Foundation

---

### Purpose

Define how the Foundation is governed, maintained, reviewed, and evolved throughout the lifecycle of the CWC Production Bible.

This section establishes governance rules to ensure that the Foundation remains stable while allowing controlled evolution.

---

### Governance Statement

The Foundation SHALL be treated as the architectural authority of the CWC Production Bible.

Changes to the Foundation SHALL occur only through governed decision-making and SHALL preserve architectural integrity.

---

### Governance Principles

#### PB-FND-GOV-001

Principle:

Stability Before Change

Statement:

The Foundation SHOULD remain stable.

Changes SHALL only be introduced when they provide clear architectural value.

---

#### PB-FND-GOV-002

Principle:

Controlled Evolution

Statement:

The Foundation MAY evolve over time through documented and approved revisions.

Evolution SHALL improve the production system without breaking canonical consistency.

---

#### PB-FND-GOV-003

Principle:

Backward Compatibility

Statement:

New Foundation standards SHOULD preserve compatibility with existing production standards whenever reasonably possible.

If compatibility cannot be preserved, migration guidance SHALL be documented.

---

#### PB-FND-GOV-004

Principle:

Canonical Authority

Statement:

The Foundation SHALL remain the authoritative source for foundational production concepts.

Downstream documents SHALL inherit from the Foundation rather than redefining it.

---

#### PB-FND-GOV-005

Principle:

Evidence-Based Governance

Statement:

Major architectural changes SHOULD be supported by operational experience, identified needs, or documented decisions.

---

### Change Management

Every Foundation change SHOULD include:

- Purpose of the change.
- Rationale.
- Expected impact.
- Affected sections.
- Version update.
- Change history entry.

---

### Versioning

The Foundation SHALL use semantic versioning.

Version increments SHOULD follow these rules:

- Major version for architectural changes.
- Minor version for new canonical capabilities.
- Patch version for clarifications, corrections, or editorial improvements.

---

## PB-CON-007 — Editorial Tone of Voice

Status: LOCK
Version: 1.0
Type: Content System

---

### Purpose

Define the canonical Editorial Tone of Voice used across all Coz We Care publications.

The Tone of Voice establishes how Coz We Care communicates with its audience, ensuring every publication is recognisable, trustworthy, approachable, and aligned with the organisation's mission.

---

### Tone of Voice Statement

Every Coz We Care publication SHALL communicate in a manner that is informative, empathetic, respectful, inclusive, and empowering.

The tone SHALL make complex health information easier to understand while maintaining scientific integrity and professional credibility.

---

### Core Voice Characteristics

#### PB-CON-TOV-001

### Clear

Communication SHALL be:

- Easy to understand.
- Well structured.
- Free from unnecessary jargon.
- Suitable for the intended audience.

Complex concepts SHOULD be explained using plain language whenever possible.

---

#### PB-CON-TOV-002

### Empathetic

Communication SHALL:

- Respect lived experiences.
- Demonstrate compassion.
- Avoid blame.
- Avoid shame.
- Avoid judgement.

Readers SHOULD feel supported rather than criticised.

---

#### PB-CON-TOV-003

### Evidence-Based

Statements SHALL reflect:

- Reliable evidence.
- Scientific consensus.
- Responsible interpretation.
- Accurate health communication.

Confidence SHALL never exceed the available evidence.

---

#### PB-CON-TOV-004

### Inclusive

Communication SHALL:

- Welcome diverse audiences.
- Respect differences.
- Avoid stereotypes.
- Use person-centred language.
- Promote equality and dignity.

---

#### PB-CON-TOV-005

### Encouraging

Content SHOULD motivate audiences to:

- Learn.
- Ask questions.
- Seek professional healthcare.
- Make informed decisions.
- Support others.

Encouragement SHALL never rely on fear or coercion.

---

#### PB-CON-TOV-006

### Professional

Editorial language SHALL remain:

- Credible.
- Responsible.
- Balanced.
- Objective.
- Consistent.

Professionalism SHALL coexist with warmth and accessibility.

---

### Communication Style

Coz We Care content SHOULD:

- Explain rather than lecture.
- Guide rather than command.
- Inform rather than alarm.
- Encourage rather than pressure.
- Educate rather than persuade aggressively.

The audience SHALL be treated as capable partners in learning.

---

### Language Guidelines

Editorial language SHOULD be:

- Simple.
- Direct.
- Conversational.
- Respectful.
- Positive.
- Human-centred.

The use of sensational, exaggerated, or emotionally manipulative language SHALL be avoided.

---

### Tone Adaptation

The Tone of Voice MAY be adjusted according to:

- Audience.
- Platform.
- Campaign.
- Publication format.

However, every adaptation SHALL preserve the Core Voice Characteristics defined in this section.

---

### Prohibited Tone

The following communication styles SHALL NOT be used in Coz We Care publications:

- Fear-based messaging.
- Stigmatising language.
- Mocking or sarcastic language.
- Discriminatory language.
- Moral judgement.
- Aggressive persuasion.
- Clickbait exaggeration.
- Unsupported certainty.

---

### AI Tone Consistency

AI-assisted content SHALL follow the same Tone of Voice standards as human-created content.

Editors SHALL verify that AI-generated language remains:

- Clear.
- Empathetic.
- Accurate.
- Inclusive.
- Consistent with the Coz We Care brand.

---

### Success Criteria

The Tone of Voice is considered successfully implemented when:

- Audiences consistently recognise the Coz We Care communication style.
- Publications remain approachable without sacrificing credibility.
- Sensitive topics are communicated respectfully.
- Human-created and AI-assisted content share the same editorial voice.

## PB-CON-008 — Writing Standards

Status: LOCK
Version: 1.0
Type: Content System

---

### Purpose

Define the canonical Writing Standards for all Coz We Care publications.

These standards establish the rules for writing content that is accurate, accessible, engaging, and consistent with the Coz We Care Editorial Philosophy, Editorial Principles, and Tone of Voice.

---

### Writing Standards Statement

Every Coz We Care publication SHALL follow a unified writing standard regardless of author, platform, language, or production method.

Writing SHALL prioritise understanding, educational value, and public trust above stylistic preference.

---

## PB-CON-WS-001

### Audience-First Writing

Content SHALL be written from the perspective of the audience.

Writers SHOULD consider:

- What the audience already knows.
- What the audience needs to know.
- What action the audience should be able to take after reading.

Writing SHALL answer audience needs before organisational objectives.

---

## PB-CON-WS-002

### Plain Language

Health information SHALL be communicated using language that is:

- Clear.
- Simple.
- Direct.
- Easy to understand.

Medical terminology MAY be used when necessary but SHALL always be explained in accessible language.

---

## PB-CON-WS-003

### Evidence-Based Statements

All factual statements SHALL:

- Be supported by reliable evidence.
- Reflect current scientific understanding.
- Avoid exaggeration.
- Avoid speculation presented as fact.

Where evidence is evolving, wording SHOULD communicate appropriate uncertainty.

---

## PB-CON-WS-004

### Person-Centred Language

Writing SHALL prioritise respectful, person-centred communication.

Examples include:

- "People living with HIV" instead of "HIV sufferers."
- "People at risk of HIV" instead of labels that define individuals by behaviour or condition.

Language SHALL recognise the dignity of every individual.

---

## PB-CON-WS-005

### Positive and Empowering Communication

Writing SHOULD encourage informed decision-making by:

- Highlighting available options.
- Explaining achievable actions.
- Reinforcing personal agency.
- Supporting healthier choices.

Content SHALL avoid fear-based or coercive messaging.

---

## PB-CON-WS-006

### Structural Consistency

Publications SHOULD follow a logical structure:

1. Capture attention.
2. Introduce the topic.
3. Explain the key information.
4. Reinforce the core message.
5. Provide practical guidance where appropriate.
6. Conclude with a clear takeaway or call to action.

The exact implementation MAY vary according to publication format.

---

## PB-CON-WS-007

### Readability

Writing SHOULD use:

- Short paragraphs.
- Logical sequencing.
- Descriptive headings.
- Bullet lists where appropriate.
- Clear transitions between ideas.

Content SHOULD minimise unnecessary complexity.

---

## PB-CON-WS-008

### Consistent Terminology

Approved editorial terminology SHALL be used consistently across all publications.

Equivalent concepts SHOULD be described using the same preferred terms unless audience adaptation requires otherwise.

Terminology standards SHALL be governed by future canonical terminology references.

---

## PB-CON-WS-009

### Call to Action

Where appropriate, publications SHOULD conclude with guidance that encourages positive health behaviour.

Calls to action SHALL be:

- Relevant.
- Ethical.
- Practical.
- Consistent with public health objectives.

Calls to action SHALL never manipulate or pressure audiences.

---

## PB-CON-WS-010

### AI Writing Compliance

AI-assisted writing SHALL comply with every Writing Standard defined in this section.

Editors SHALL verify that AI-generated content demonstrates:

- Clarity.
- Accuracy.
- Respectful language.
- Editorial consistency.
- Appropriate educational quality.

AI-generated text SHALL always remain subject to human editorial approval before publication.

---

### Success Criteria

The Writing Standards are considered successfully implemented when:

- Publications are consistently easy to understand.

- Editorial language reflects respect and empathy.
- Human-created and AI-assisted content follow the same writing framework.

## PB-CON-009 — Scientific Accuracy Standards

Status: LOCK
Version: 1.0
Type: Content System

---

### Purpose

Define the canonical Scientific Accuracy Standards governing all health information published by Coz We Care.

These standards ensure that every publication reflects current scientific knowledge, supports public health objectives, and maintains the trust of audiences through accurate, responsible, and evidence-based communication.

---

### Scientific Accuracy Statement

All scientific information published under the Coz We Care brand SHALL be accurate, verifiable, evidence-based, and presented within its appropriate context.

Scientific integrity SHALL always take precedence over engagement, storytelling, or stylistic considerations.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-001

### Evidence Hierarchy

Editorial decisions SHALL prioritise evidence according to the following hierarchy:

1. International clinical guidelines.
2. National public health guidelines.
3. Peer-reviewed systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
4. Peer-reviewed scientific research.
5. Consensus statements from recognised professional organisations.
6. Official public health statistics.
7. Expert opinion supported by evidence.

Lower levels of evidence SHALL NOT override higher-quality evidence without explicit justification.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-002

### Approved Information Sources

Scientific information SHOULD primarily originate from recognised authorities, including:

- World Health Organization (WHO).
- UNAIDS.
- Indonesian Ministry of Health.
- United States Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).
- European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (ECDC).
- International AIDS Society (IAS).
- International Antiviral Society–USA (IAS–USA).
- Peer-reviewed scientific journals.

Equivalent authoritative organisations MAY also be used where appropriate.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-003

### Fact Verification

Before publication, factual claims SHALL be verified for:

- Accuracy.
- Current validity.
- Context.
- Appropriate interpretation.
- Consistency with recognised scientific evidence.

Information SHALL NOT be published solely because it is widely shared or commonly believed.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-004

### Scientific Context

Scientific findings SHALL be presented with sufficient context to prevent misunderstanding.

Editors SHOULD explain:

- What is known.
- What remains uncertain.
- The practical implications for the audience.
- Any important limitations.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-005

### Health Claims

Health-related claims SHALL:

- Be supported by evidence.
- Avoid guarantees.
- Avoid misleading certainty.
- Avoid exaggerated benefits.
- Avoid unsupported risks.

Where evidence is limited, this SHALL be clearly communicated.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-006

### Medical Terminology

Medical terminology SHALL:

- Follow recognised clinical usage.
- Be applied consistently.
- Be explained using accessible language where necessary.

Terminology SHOULD balance scientific precision with public understanding.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-007

### Updating Scientific Information

Scientific content SHOULD be reviewed periodically to ensure continued accuracy.

When significant scientific developments occur:

- Publications MAY be updated.
- Outdated guidance SHOULD be revised.
- Superseded information SHALL NOT continue to be presented as current.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-008

### Managing Scientific Uncertainty

Where evidence is evolving, editorial language SHOULD:

- Describe current knowledge accurately.
- Acknowledge uncertainty.
- Avoid speculation.
- Distinguish evidence from hypothesis.

Transparency strengthens public trust.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-009

### AI Scientific Validation

AI-generated scientific content SHALL undergo human review before publication.

Editors SHALL verify:

- Accuracy.
- Current relevance.
- Correct interpretation.
- Consistency with recognised scientific evidence.

AI SHALL never be treated as the primary scientific authority.

---

## PB-CON-SAS-010

### Correction Policy

If a scientific error is identified after publication:

- The error SHALL be corrected promptly.
- Updated information SHALL replace inaccurate information.
- Significant corrections SHOULD be communicated transparently where appropriate.

Maintaining public trust SHALL take priority over preserving historical publications unchanged.

---

### Success Criteria

The Scientific Accuracy Standards are considered successfully implemented when:

- Publications consistently reflect current scientific understanding.
- Health information is evidence-based and responsibly communicated.
- Scientific uncertainty is explained transparently.
- Audiences can rely on Coz We Care as a trustworthy educational source.

---

### Purpose

Define the canonical Editorial Glossary and Controlled Vocabulary used throughout Coz We Care publications.

This framework ensures that terminology is applied consistently across all editorial assets, reducing ambiguity while improving scientific accuracy, readability, and brand consistency.

### Controlled Vocabulary Statement

All editorial publications SHALL use approved terminology wherever applicable.

Controlled vocabulary exists to ensure consistency in communication, strengthen audience understanding, and maintain scientific and editorial integrity.

---

## PB-CON-GLO-001

### Objectives

The Editorial Glossary exists to:

- Standardise terminology.
- Improve editorial consistency.
- Reduce ambiguity.
- Support scientific accuracy.
- Improve readability.
- Assist AI-assisted production.
- Support long-term knowledge management.

## PB-CON-GLO-002

The Editorial Glossary MAY include:

- HIV-related terminology.

- Editorial terminology.
- Production terminology.
- Governance terminology.
