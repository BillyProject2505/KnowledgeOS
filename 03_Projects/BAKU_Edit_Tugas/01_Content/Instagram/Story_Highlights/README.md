## Status Definitions

- `NOT STARTED` — production has not begun.
- `WORKING` — production is actively underway.
- `DRAFT` — draft exists but is not approved.
- `APPROVED` — production/content has been explicitly approved for the relevant gate.
- `PUBLISHED` — the Highlight or Story has been published on Instagram.
- `SUPERSEDED` — previously valid state/content replaced by a later explicit decision.
- `OPEN` — requires an explicit decision or required information.
- `BLOCKED` — production cannot proceed without required information/assets.

## Rules

- A Highlight groups one or more Stories under one Instagram Highlight.
- A checkmark/status must reflect the actual production state; do not infer publication from design completion alone.
- Future Highlight content is not canonical until the required content scope and copy are explicitly approved.
- Genuine testimonials/social proof must be evidence-backed.
- Visual production must follow the reusable Instagram Story Production Standard and any approved series-specific baseline.
