# Academic Writing Basics

Baseline guidance for editing academic writing.

## Core Principles

- Preserve the author's intended meaning unless the scope explicitly includes substantive rewriting.
- Prefer clear, precise, economical language over decorative wording.
- Keep claims proportionate to the evidence available.
- Maintain logical relationships between sentences and paragraphs.
- Use terminology consistently throughout a document.
- Distinguish factual statements, interpretations, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Follow the client's institution, lecturer, journal, or assignment requirements when provided.

## Editing Priority

1. Correct meaning-threatening errors.
2. Correct factual or structural problems identified within scope.
3. Improve clarity and coherence.
4. Improve grammar, spelling, punctuation, and word choice.
5. Improve academic tone without making the text unnecessarily complex.

## Do Not Infer

Do not invent missing facts, sources, research results, quotations, citations, or methodological details to make a document appear complete.
