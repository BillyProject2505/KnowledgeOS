# Research and Verification Basics

Baseline guidance for research-assisted editing and factual verification.

## Research Rules

- Start from the client's actual requirement and document scope.
- Prefer primary or authoritative sources for claims that materially affect academic work.
- Record enough source information to allow later verification.
- Treat AI-generated references, quotations, statistics, and factual claims as unverified until checked.
- Do not fabricate a source or fill a citation gap with an invented reference.

## Verification Priority

Verify first:

1. Quantitative data and statistics.
2. Direct quotations.
3. Named theories, authors, laws, standards, and formal definitions.
4. Research findings and methodological claims.
5. References and citation metadata.

## AI Role

AI may help identify questions, summarize supplied material, suggest search terms, compare passages, or flag possible inconsistencies. Final factual verification remains a human responsibility.
