# BAKU Originality and AI Assistance Policy

## Purpose
Define how BAKU handles plagiarism/similarity screening and AI-assistance assessment without overstating what any detection method can prove.

## Core Principles
- Similarity is not automatically plagiarism.
- AI-writing indicators are not proof of AI authorship.
- No detector score may be treated as a standalone academic misconduct finding.
- Findings must be reported with evidence, scope, and uncertainty.
- Human review is required before a conclusion is communicated to the client.

## Originality Review
When originality checking is requested or required:
1. Identify the document sections to be checked.
2. Compare relevant text against available source material or a trusted similarity service.
3. Record exact or substantially similar passages when evidence is available.
4. Determine whether similarity has an apparent legitimate basis, such as quotations, references, standard terminology, or common phrasing.
5. Flag unresolved similarity for client review when a definitive determination cannot be made.

## AI-Assistance Review
AI-authorship or AI-paraphrase screening may consider linguistic indicators such as formulaic phrasing, repetitive structure, abrupt style shifts, and other anomalies. These indicators are signals for review, not proof.

## Reporting Categories
- No material issue observed — no significant evidence found within the checked scope.
- Similarity requires review — potentially overlapping text was identified, but context is needed.
- AI-assistance indicators observed — stylistic signals suggest further human review may be useful.
- Evidence insufficient — available information does not support a reliable conclusion.

## Prohibited Claims
Do not report statements such as:
- "This document is definitely AI-generated."
- "AI score proves plagiarism."
- "0% AI means human-written."
- "A similarity percentage alone proves plagiarism."

## Confidentiality
Do not upload confidential client documents to external checking services unless the service is approved for the intended data and the handling is consistent with BAKU's confidentiality requirements.
