# Story Highlight Visual Production Protocol

**Status:** Canonical Production Protocol v1.0  
**Scope:** Instagram Story Highlight — BAKU Edit Tugas  
**Primary reference:** Approved Story 1 visual baseline in `01_Content/Instagram/Story_Highlight_FAQ/CONTENT.md`

## Purpose

Standardize AI-assisted visual production for BAKU Edit Tugas Story Highlight content without allowing the image generator to redefine the approved Story system.

## Core Principle

> **AI generates the visual asset. Canva assembles the communication.**

The approved Story 1 artifact is the visual baseline. Stories 2–4 may change scene, characters, objects, and visual metaphor, but must preserve the established visual system.

## Locked Story Format

- Instagram Story static format.
- 9:16.
- Target size: 1080 × 1920 px.
- Flat editorial illustration language.
- Warm cream/off-white background relationship.
- Deep navy primary brand relationship.
- Warm gold accent relationship.
- Typography, logo placement, exact spacing, alignment, copy, CTA treatment, and final QC are completed in Canva.

## AI MAY Generate

- Story-specific editorial illustration.
- Student/customer characters.
- Academic workspace scenes.
- Laptop, documents, books, notes, desk objects, workflow/time/security/online-consultation metaphors.
- Supporting decorative elements that fit the approved illustration language.
- Lighting, texture, depth, and restrained background treatment consistent with the approved Story 1 baseline.
- Composition that intentionally leaves usable space for Canva typography and UI elements.

## AI MUST NOT Generate

- BAKU Edit Tugas logo or replacement logo.
- Readable final copy.
- Question/hook text.
- Answer text.
- Supporting copy.
- CTA or WhatsApp/contact information.
- Final typography.
- Speech-bubble text.
- Note-card text.
- Final Story layout.
- Fake testimonials, statistics, customer counts, privacy/security claims, or other unsupported claims.
- Instagram UI or simulated Highlight interface.
- Any invented brand asset.

## Composition Boundary

The image generator must create a **visual asset**, not a finished Story slide.

Required composition behavior:

1. Preserve the approved Story 1 visual language.
2. Establish one clear focal subject or metaphor.
3. Leave sufficient negative space for Canva copy and brand elements.
4. Do not place important visual details where the approved Story typography will sit.
5. Do not simulate the complete Story 1 frame.
6. Do not reproduce logo placement as part of the generated image.
7. Do not create UI-like text cards, CTA buttons, or text-bearing interface elements.
8. Avoid unnecessary visual complexity that competes with the message.

## Generation Prompt Pattern

Use this structure when generating a Story Highlight visual:

> Create a flat editorial illustration for a BAKU Edit Tugas Instagram Story Highlight. The visual should communicate [STORY PURPOSE / VISUAL METAPHOR]. Use an approachable academic context with [SUBJECTS / OBJECTS]. Match the established BAKU Edit Tugas Story 1 visual baseline: warm cream/off-white background relationship, deep navy primary relationship, warm gold accent relationship, clean flat editorial illustration, friendly and professional academic atmosphere. Compose the scene vertically for 9:16 and preserve generous clean negative space in [INTENDED CANVA TEXT AREA]. Keep the composition simple, readable, and compatible with a Canva-built Story frame.
>
> **IMPORTANT: Create only the visual asset. Do not create the final Story slide.**
>
> **DO NOT GENERATE:** BAKU Edit Tugas logo, readable text, typography, question/hook, answer copy, supporting copy, CTA, WhatsApp/contact information, speech-bubble text, note-card text, information cards, footer text, Instagram UI, or final Story layout.

## Post-Generation Visual QC

Reject or regenerate if the output:

- looks like a complete poster/Story rather than a visual asset;
- contains a generated BAKU logo or wordmark;
- contains incorrect or unwanted readable text;
- introduces a claim or implication not present in approved content;
- conflicts with the approved flat editorial illustration language;
- leaves insufficient space for the approved Canva composition;
- becomes visually too busy for Story readability;
- contains artifacts, distorted anatomy/objects, watermarks, or obvious generation errors;
- changes the established visual DNA merely for novelty.

## Canva Handoff

After AI Visual QC passes:

1. Place the visual asset into the approved Story 1-derived frame.
2. Add the canonical BAKU Edit Tugas logo manually.
3. Insert approved Story copy exactly.
4. Apply typography treatment from the approved Story 1 Canva implementation.
5. Build speech bubbles, note cards, CTA, or other communication components in Canva as required by the approved content.
6. Verify safe area, spacing, hierarchy, and alignment.
7. Run Final QC.
8. Export at 1080 × 1920 px.

## Production Rule

A Story Highlight visual is **not production-complete** when the AI image is approved. Completion requires Canva composition and Final QC.
