# Story Highlight Visual QC Checklist

**Status:** Canonical QC Checklist v1.0  
**Scope:** AI-generated visual assets for BAKU Edit Tugas Story Highlight

## A. Generation Boundary

- [ ] Output is a visual asset, not a finished Story slide.
- [ ] No BAKU Edit Tugas logo or replacement wordmark.
- [ ] No final readable copy.
- [ ] No generated typography.
- [ ] No CTA or WhatsApp/contact information.
- [ ] No speech-bubble/note-card text.
- [ ] No footer or Instagram UI.
- [ ] No fabricated claims, statistics, testimonials, or social proof.

## B. Story Relevance

- [ ] Visual clearly supports the approved Story purpose.
- [ ] Visual metaphor is understandable without adding a new claim.
- [ ] Character/action/object context is appropriate for students and academic needs.
- [ ] Visual does not imply a service, guarantee, or condition that is absent from approved content.

## C. Brand & Visual System

- [ ] Flat editorial illustration language is maintained.
- [ ] Overall feel is Professional + Modern + Academic + Approachable.
- [ ] Warm cream/off-white, deep navy, and warm gold relationships are compatible with the approved Story 1 baseline.
- [ ] Visual does not introduce an unrelated style merely for novelty.
- [ ] The visual can sit naturally inside the approved Story 1-derived frame.

## D. Composition

- [ ] One clear focal point.
- [ ] Story-specific visual hierarchy is readable.
- [ ] Sufficient negative space exists for Canva typography and communication components.
- [ ] Important visual details do not occupy the intended text area.
- [ ] Composition works at 9:16 / 1080 × 1920 px.
- [ ] Cropping will not remove essential visual meaning.

## E. Technical Quality

- [ ] No obvious AI artifacts.
- [ ] No distorted anatomy or academic objects.
- [ ] No accidental watermarks.
- [ ] No unintended symbols or branding.
- [ ] Resolution is sufficient for final Story production.

## Decision

**ACCEPT** — ready for Canva composition.  
**REGENERATE** — concept is correct but visual execution fails one or more checks.  
**REJECT** — visual concept conflicts with content, brand, or production boundary.

## Important

AI Visual QC approval is **not** final production approval. The approved visual must still pass Canva composition, Final QC, and export checks.
