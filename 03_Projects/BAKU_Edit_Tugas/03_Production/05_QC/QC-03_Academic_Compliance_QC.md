## 1. Purpose

Memverifikasi bahwa academic compliance dan source verification telah dilakukan berdasarkan source of truth yang berlaku, applicability telah dinilai, evidence cukup untuk verdict, dan unresolved issues tetap visible.

## 2. Applicability

Wajib ketika pekerjaan mencakup institutional/assignment compliance, source verification, citation/claim verification, atau academic quality review yang bergantung pada evidence.

## 4. Compliance Gate Decision

Choose one:

- `PASS`
- `REVIEW`
- `BLOCKED`

### Gate rule

`PASS` requires all applicable requirements in scope to have evidence-backed status, no unresolved blocking issue, and required source verification to be complete.

Use `REVIEW` when evidence or applicability is incomplete but non-compliance is not established.

Use `BLOCKED` when a material compliance/verification dependency prevents a reliable result or when a blocking substantive issue remains unresolved.

## 5. Failure / Escalation Checks

Block or route to resolution when:

- the claimed institutional guideline cannot be validated;
- a compliance verdict relies on insufficient or inapplicable evidence;
- a `FAIL` is asserted without enough evidence;
- a material source cannot be verified and the claim depends on it;
- a substantive academic issue requires an unrecorded human/author decision.

Do not repair the QC record by changing a finding status without new evidence or a valid decision.
