# BAKU Formatting Standard

## Purpose
Provide a baseline for document presentation and formatting during editing work.

## Scope
Formatting must follow the client's institution, lecturer, journal, template, or other explicit requirements when provided.

## Baseline Checks
Review as applicable:
- page size and margins;
- font family and size;
- line spacing;
- paragraph spacing and indentation;
- heading hierarchy;
- page numbering;
- tables and figures;
- captions;
- header and footer;
- references and bibliography formatting;
- alignment and list formatting.

## Priority Rule
Client-provided requirements override BAKU's baseline preferences. When requirements conflict or are unclear, document the issue before making a consequential change.

## Quality Rule
Formatting should improve readability and compliance without introducing cosmetic complexity that serves no functional purpose.

## Final Check
The final document should be opened and visually inspected after formatting changes. Do not rely solely on source-level or automated checks.
