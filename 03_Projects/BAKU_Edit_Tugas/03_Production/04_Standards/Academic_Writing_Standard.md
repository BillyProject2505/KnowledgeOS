# BAKU Academic Writing Standard

## Purpose
Provide practical standards for improving academic writing without imposing an artificial or unsupported style.

## Clarity
- Prefer direct, precise sentences.
- Remove ambiguity where the intended meaning is clear.
- Avoid unnecessary filler and repetition.
- Preserve technical meaning and discipline-specific terminology.

## Academic Tone
Writing should be formal, objective, and appropriate to the academic context. Do not make text artificially complex merely to sound academic.

## Structure
Paragraphs should have a clear function. Arguments should progress logically, with evidence supporting substantive claims where required.

## Coherence
Check transitions, reference words, terminology, and logical relationships between sentences and paragraphs.

## Voice and Authorship
Preserve the author's voice and intellectual ownership unless a broader rewriting scope is explicitly requested.

## Evidence and Claims
Do not strengthen a claim beyond what the available evidence supports. Flag unsupported or unclear claims rather than inventing support.

## AI Boundary
AI may suggest revisions, but final academic judgment remains with the human reviewer.
