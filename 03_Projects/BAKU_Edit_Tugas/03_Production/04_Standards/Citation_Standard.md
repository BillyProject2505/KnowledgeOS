# BAKU Citation Standard

## Purpose
Set the minimum standard for handling citations and references in AI-assisted academic editing.

## Core Rule
A citation must support the claim it is attached to, and a reference must be traceable to a real source.

## Verification
Before final delivery:
- verify that cited sources exist;
- verify author, title, publication, and year where material to the task;
- verify that the source actually supports the claim;
- preserve the client's required citation style unless a change is in scope;
- flag incomplete or unverifiable references.

## AI Restrictions
Never accept an AI-generated citation solely because it looks plausible. AI may propose search terms or candidate references, but source verification is required.

## Missing Evidence
When evidence cannot be verified:
- do not fabricate a replacement;
- mark the issue clearly;
- request or locate an appropriate source when the agreed scope permits.

## Final QC
No document should be marked ready for delivery while a known critical citation integrity issue remains unresolved or explicitly communicated to the client.
