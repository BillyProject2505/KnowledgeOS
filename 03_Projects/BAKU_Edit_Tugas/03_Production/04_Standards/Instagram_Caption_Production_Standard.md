# BAKU Edit Tugas — Instagram Caption Production Standard

**Status:** Canonical Production Standard  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Scope:** Instagram captions for BAKU Edit Tugas  
**Primary platform context:** Instagram / Meta ecosystem

---

## 1. Purpose

Define a repeatable caption standard that supports BAKU Edit Tugas brand positioning, local discoverability, engagement, inquiry, and conversion on Instagram.

This standard complements the Instagram poster standards. The poster communicates the core proposition visually; the caption supports discovery, context, engagement, and action.

---

## 2. Brand Context

- **Brand:** BAKU Edit Tugas
- **Tagline:** Rapi • Jelas • Siap
- **Location:** Manado, Sulawesi Utara
- **WhatsApp Business:** 0897-7706-838
- **Primary audience:** students and university students in Manado / North Sulawesi
- **Relevant services:** Review Skripsi, Proofreading, Editing Grammar, Formatting, Sitasi & Referensi, Konsultasi Tulis

---

## 3. Tone and Voice

Target voice:

> **70% professional + 30% casual Bahasa Manado**

Characteristics:
- friendly;
- calm;
- natural;
- reassuring;
- not condescending;
- not arrogant;
- locally recognizable without becoming difficult to understand.

Common Manado expressions may include words such as **so, jo, boleh jo, mar, torang**, when they sound natural in context.

Do not overuse dialect simply to appear local.

---

## 4. Ethical Positioning

BAKU must not present its academic services as cheating, impersonation, or guaranteed academic outcomes.

Use the following positioning consistently when relevant:

> **Bukan joki, kamu tetap penulisnya, torang bantu bekeng lebe baku.**

For proofreading, preserve the principle:

> **Tanpa mengubah makna dan maksud penulis.**

When AI assistance is relevant, disclose the service model as:

> **Berbantuan AI + bimbingan manual.**

Do not claim that AI completes the academic work independently or fully on behalf of the client.

Prohibited positioning includes:
- joki skripsi;
- jasa buat skripsi;
- terima beres;
- guaranteed grades;
- guaranteed graduation;
- guaranteed acceptance or approval.

---

## 5. Mandatory Caption Structure

Use this order unless a campaign-specific exception is explicitly approved:

### 1. Hook
First 1–2 lines.

Use a relatable question/problem and include **Manado** or **Sulut** where natural.

Example:
> **SKRIPSI SO MENTOK? 😵‍💫 Khusus Mahasiswa Manado 📚**

### 2. Pain
Validate the user's problem in one concise sentence.

### 3. Solution
Introduce the specific BAKU service in one sentence.

### 4. Benefits
Use three concise points, preferably with:
- ✍️
- 💡
- 📑

Explain what is checked, reviewed, or improved — not what is "done for" the client.

### 5. Ethical Disclaimer
State the collaboration/ownership principle clearly.

### 6. Comment CTA
Always include a comment-based CTA when the campaign objective is engagement or lead generation.

Example:
> **Komen MENTOK / CEK, nanti torang DM checklist gratis!**

Do not make the WhatsApp CTA the only action in a lead-generation caption.

### 7. WhatsApp CTA
Place the primary contact action near the end.

Default:
> **Klik tombol Contact di bio / WA Bisnis: 0897-7706-838**

### 8. Tagline
> **Rapi • Jelas • Siap**

### 9. Hashtags
Use **6 hyper-local, service-specific hashtags** by default.

Recommended pattern:
1. `#BAKUEditTugas`
2. `#[JenisLayanan]Manado`
3. `#Konsultasi[Jenis]Manado` where semantically appropriate
4. `#MahasiswaManado`
5. `#SkripsiSulut`
6. `#Manado`

Service-specific hashtag spelling must remain readable and relevant.

Avoid generic or misleading hashtags such as:
- `#JokiSkripsi`
- `#JasaBuatSkripsi`
- `#SkripsiMahasiswa`
- `#Mahasiswa`
when they do not add useful targeting value.

---

## 6. Caption Length

Target concise, readable captions. A working target is approximately **120–150 words**, but this is a production guideline rather than a platform guarantee.

Do not add filler solely to reach a word count.

---

## 7. Local SEO and Discoverability

Prefer natural inclusion of:
- Manado;
- Sulut / Sulawesi Utara;
- student/student audience terms;
- the exact service name;
- problem-specific keywords.

Local keywords should appear naturally in the hook and/or body. Do not keyword-stuff.

The exact service name should normally appear in the caption so the content remains semantically aligned with the poster and service catalog.

---

## 8. CTA Rules

For engagement or lead-generation content, use two actions:

**Primary engagement action:** comment CTA.  
**Primary conversion action:** WhatsApp / Contact in bio.

The comment CTA should be simple and relevant to the problem in the post.

Do not use aggressive engagement bait unrelated to the content.

---

## 9. Emoji Rules

Use emojis as navigation, emphasis, or tone markers.

Preferred approach:
- 1–2 emojis in the hook;
- limited emojis for benefit bullets;
- avoid decorative emoji overload;
- do not substitute important words with emojis.

---

## 10. Service-Specific Accuracy

The caption must match the approved service scope.

Examples:

**Proofreading**
- typo, spelling, punctuation;
- grammar and clarity;
- terminology, consistency, coherence;
- readability and flow.

**Review Skripsi**
- structure and chapter flow;
- relationship between title, research questions, method, discussion, conclusion;
- feedback and consultation within approved scope.

**Editing Grammar**
- spelling;
- punctuation;
- grammar and syntax;
- language correctness.

Do not merge services in a way that makes their propositions indistinguishable unless the campaign explicitly promotes a bundled service.

---

## 11. Accuracy and Claim Control

Do not invent:
- turnaround times;
- revision counts;
- prices;
- guarantees;
- institutional compliance claims;
- academic outcomes.

When a specific offer or operational promise exists, it must be traceable to the current approved service information.

---

## 12. Platform-Change Note

Instagram/Meta recommendations, distribution behavior, search behavior, and platform limits can change.

Therefore:
- platform-specific claims are **operational guidance, not immutable rules**;
- time-sensitive claims about algorithms, ranking, or limits must be re-verified before being treated as factual standards;
- this document should be updated when reliable platform guidance materially changes.

Do not encode speculative algorithm folklore as a permanent BAKU rule.

## 14. Caption QC Checklist

Before publishing, verify:

### Brand
- [ ] Tone is 70% professional / 30% casual Manado.
- [ ] Brand positioning is clear.
- [ ] No joki/terima-beres language.

### Content
- [ ] Exact service name is correct.
- [ ] Hook is relatable.
- [ ] Problem is clear.
- [ ] Benefits describe real checks/improvements.
- [ ] Ethical positioning is present.
- [ ] No unsupported claims.

### Discovery
- [ ] Manado/Sulut appears naturally where relevant.
- [ ] Service keyword is present.
- [ ] Hashtags are limited to 6 and locally relevant.

### Conversion
- [ ] Comment CTA is present when required.
- [ ] WhatsApp CTA is present.
- [ ] Number is exactly **0897-7706-838**.

### Readability
- [ ] Concise.
- [ ] No unnecessary repetition.
- [ ] Emojis are functional, not excessive.
- [ ] Caption supports the poster rather than merely repeating it.
