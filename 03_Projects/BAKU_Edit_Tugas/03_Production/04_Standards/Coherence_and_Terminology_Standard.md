# BAKU Coherence and Terminology Standard

## Purpose
Ensure that edited documents read as one coherent work and use terminology consistently.

## Coherence
Check that:
- each paragraph has a clear relationship to the surrounding text;
- transitions reflect the actual logical relationship;
- pronouns and references have clear antecedents;
- headings match the content that follows;
- conclusions are supported by the preceding discussion.

## Terminology
- Use one preferred term for the same concept unless a distinction is intentional.
- Preserve discipline-specific terminology.
- Do not replace a technical term with a generic synonym when precision would be lost.
- Define uncommon or central terms when required by the document context.

## Consistency Checks
Review consistency of:
- names and labels;
- abbreviations;
- capitalization;
- numbers and units;
- tense where relevant;
- heading hierarchy;
- references to tables, figures, and sections.

## AI Boundary
AI may identify possible inconsistencies, but the reviewer must determine whether apparently different terms are genuinely equivalent in context.
