# BAKU Academic Compliance Standard

## Purpose
Define the minimum standard for checking whether an academic document is structurally and linguistically appropriate for its stated academic purpose.

## Scope
This standard applies when BAKU reviews academic documents such as assignments, papers, proposals, theses, research reports, and similar work.

## Core Principle
Academic compliance must be assessed against the client's applicable institutional, faculty, program, lecturer, journal, or assignment guidelines when those guidelines are provided.

## Assessment Order
1. Identify the applicable guideline or requirement.
2. Map the document structure against the requirement.
3. Check language and academic register.
4. Check methodological or disciplinary consistency when within scope.
5. Identify missing, inconsistent, or non-compliant elements.
6. Record issues and recommendations.

## Structure Review
Check, as applicable:
- chapter/section order;
- heading hierarchy;
- logical progression;
- required components;
- numbering consistency;
- alignment with the document type.

## Language Review
Check:
- grammatical correctness;
- sentence clarity;
- paragraph coherence;
- terminology consistency;
- academic register;
- unnecessary repetition;
- ambiguity.

## Guideline Priority
When a specific institutional or client guideline exists, it takes precedence over BAKU's generic baseline.

## Limits
BAKU must not claim that a document is compliant with a specific university or program standard when the applicable official guideline has not been provided or verified.

## Output
The review should distinguish:
- Critical issue — must be resolved before delivery;
- Improvement — should be improved where practical;
- Preference — optional stylistic refinement.
