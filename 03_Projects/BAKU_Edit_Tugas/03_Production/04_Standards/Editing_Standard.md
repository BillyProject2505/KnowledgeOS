# BAKU Editing Standard

## Purpose
Define the minimum quality standard for editing work performed by BAKU Edit Tugas, including AI-assisted editing.

## Core Principle
Editing improves the document without changing the author's intended meaning, argument, factual content, or academic context unless the client explicitly requests substantive revision.

## Editing Priorities
1. Accuracy
2. Meaning preservation
3. Clarity
4. Coherence
5. Academic appropriateness
6. Language correctness
7. Consistency
8. Formatting

## Allowed Editing
- spelling and typographical corrections;
- grammar and syntax correction;
- sentence clarity and concision;
- paragraph coherence;
- terminology consistency;
- academic tone improvement;
- removal of unnecessary repetition;
- structural suggestions when within agreed scope;
- formatting corrections within agreed requirements.

## Restricted Editing
Do not silently:
- change the author's position or conclusion;
- introduce unsupported facts;
- fabricate evidence or citations;
- alter research results;
- change methodological claims;
- invent references;
- substantially rewrite content outside the agreed scope.

## AI Use
AI may assist with diagnosis, alternatives, language improvement, restructuring suggestions, and review. AI output must be reviewed by a human before acceptance.

## Final Acceptance Criteria
A document may proceed to final QC only when:
- requirements are satisfied;
- meaning is preserved;
- language is appropriate;
- terminology is consistent;
- factual/citation issues are verified or explicitly flagged;
- formatting requirements are satisfied;
- no unresolved critical issue remains.
