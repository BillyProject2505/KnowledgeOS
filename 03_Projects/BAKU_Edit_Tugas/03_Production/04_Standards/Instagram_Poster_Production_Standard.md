# BAKU Edit Tugas — Instagram Poster Production Standard

**Status:** Canonical Standard  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Scope:** Instagram feed poster production for BAKU Edit Tugas  
**Source of truth:** This document defines the minimum production standard for new Instagram posters and poster variants.

---

## 1. Purpose

Ensure every BAKU Edit Tugas Instagram poster is consistent, readable, production-ready, and aligned with the brand system while allowing content-specific variation.

**Principles:**
- Consistency > novelty
- Clarity > decoration
- Function > aesthetics
- Conversion > vanity
- Start simple → validate → improve → scale

---

## 2. Locked Technical Specifications

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Canvas | **1080 × 1350 px** |
| Aspect ratio | **4:5** |
| Safe margin | **60 px** on all sides |
| Safe-area size | **960 × 1230 px** |
| Primary platform | Instagram Feed |
| Finalization layer | Canva |

All critical content must remain inside the 60 px safe margin, including headline, body copy, CTA, logo, service information, and important icons.

Do not rely on AI/image-generator output for exact pixel dimensions. Use deterministic resize/export in production to enforce 1080 × 1350 px without non-uniform stretching.

## 3. Visual System

### 3.1 Master Frame

The approved Master Frame is the structural reference for Instagram posters. New posters should inherit its visual DNA and grid logic rather than copy every element literally.

A poster may vary in content and visual emphasis, but the underlying system should remain recognizable as BAKU Edit Tugas.

### 3.2 Color Direction

Primary visual palette:
- Navy / deep blue
- Gold / yellow
- Cream / off-white
- White

Use the established brand values from approved assets. Do not introduce a new dominant color system without explicit approval.

### 3.3 Typography

Use the established brand typography system and maintain clear hierarchy between:
- headline;
- subheadline/supporting statement;
- section labels;
- service titles;
- body copy;
- CTA.

Avoid excessive font variation. Prioritize readability at Instagram feed scale.

### 3.4 Shape System

All major content blocks/panels use **rounded rectangles** with a consistent radius language.

Avoid mixing rounded cards with arbitrary geometric blocks, brush-stroke panels, or unrelated shapes unless explicitly approved for a specific creative concept.

### 3.5 Iconography

Icons should be stylistically consistent in stroke/weight, visual complexity, and color treatment. Use icons to reinforce meaning, not as decoration.

## 4. Standard Poster Structure

The following structure is the default pattern and may be adapted per content objective:

1. **Header** — logo area / brand identity / service label
2. **Headline area** — primary problem or promise
3. **Visual area** — main image/illustration representing the service
4. **Information area** — service features, benefits, or supporting details
5. **CTA area** — clear next action, normally WhatsApp
6. **Trust/support area** — concise reassurance or service benefits

Not every poster must contain every section if removing a section improves clarity. Do not add sections merely to fill space.

---

## 5. Content Rules

Every poster must have one primary communication objective:
- Awareness
- Education
- Engagement
- Trust
- Lead generation
- Conversion
- Retention

Before production, define internally:
- Who is the audience?
- What problem are they experiencing?
- What is the main message?
- What solution is being offered?
- What action should the viewer take?

Use the basic copy logic:

**Problem → Solution → Benefit → CTA**

Keep copy concise. Remove wording that does not improve understanding, trust, or conversion.

### Service accuracy

Poster service names and claims must remain traceable to the approved BAKU Edit Tugas service catalog and current project standards. Do not invent services, guarantees, turnaround promises, or academic outcomes.

For the current six-service catalog, service names are:
1. Review Skripsi
2. Proofreading
3. Editing Grammar
4. Formatting
5. Sitasi & Referensi
6. Konsultasi Tulis

## 6. Logo Rules

The BAKU Edit Tugas logo is a **brand asset**, not a generative design element.

Rules:
- Do not regenerate or redraw the logo with AI.
- Do not alter its colors, proportions, or internal geometry.
- Do not stretch non-uniformly.
- Use the approved source asset.
- Logo placement may be finalized manually in Canva.
- Reserve adequate space for the logo during generation/layout.

When exact logo fidelity matters, insert the source asset during finalization rather than relying on an image generator.

## 8. Layout, Spacing, and Alignment

Use a consistent grid and spacing rhythm across sections.

Required checks:
- consistent left/right alignment;
- consistent column widths where cards are repeated;
- aligned baselines for comparable content;
- balanced vertical rhythm;
- clear separation between sections;
- no overcrowding;
- no important element touching the safe margin.

When multiple cards perform the same function, their internal structure should use comparable spacing and hierarchy.

Micro-alignment takes priority over decorative additions.

---

## 9. AI Generation Rules

AI may be used for:
- visual concept exploration;
- hero imagery;
- illustrations;
- supporting visual elements.

AI output is **not automatically final**.

Do not depend on AI for:
- exact text accuracy;
- exact logo fidelity;
- exact pixel dimensions;
- production typography;
- precise spacing/alignment.

Production workflow:

**Concept → Generation → Visual QC → Canva → Final QC → Export**

---

## 10. Final QC Checklist

### Brand
- Does the poster clearly feel like BAKU Edit Tugas?
- Is the visual system consistent with the Master Frame?
- Is the approved logo asset used correctly?

### Content
- Is the service name accurate?
- Is the primary message immediately understandable?
- Are claims supportable?
- Is the CTA clear?

### Visual
- Is hierarchy obvious?
- Is typography readable?
- Are spacing and alignment consistent?
- Are all major blocks rounded rectangles?
- Is the composition balanced?

### Technical
- Canvas = **1080 × 1350 px**
- Ratio = **4:5**
- Safe margin = **60 px**
- No non-uniform stretching
- Final export checked after Canva finalization

### Conversion
- Does the viewer understand the service?
- Is the benefit clear?
- Does the viewer know what to do next?

Classify findings as:
- **Critical issue** — must be fixed before final
- **Improvement** — should be fixed when practical
- **Preference** — optional

---

## 11. Series Consistency

Hero Service posters should function as a coherent series.

For each Hero Service:
- keep the same core visual system;
- change the service-specific message and visual;
- avoid redesigning the entire layout for novelty;
- ensure each poster has a distinct service proposition.

The Master Frame is the system. Individual Hero Service posters are applications of that system.
