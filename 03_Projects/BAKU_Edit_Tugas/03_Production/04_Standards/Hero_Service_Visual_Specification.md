# BAKU Edit Tugas — Hero Service Visual Specification

**Status:** Canonical Visual Specification  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Scope:** Instagram Hero Service poster series  
**Relationship:** Supplements `Instagram_Poster_Production_Standard.md`  
**Reference implementation:** Approved Hero Service #1 poster

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the visual application standard for the BAKU Edit Tugas Hero Service poster series.

The purpose is to ensure that Hero Service posters remain visually recognizable as one coherent series while allowing each service to have its own problem, message, and supporting visual.

The **Master Frame is the system**. Individual Hero Service posters are applications of that system.

This specification supplements the broader Instagram Poster Production Standard and does not replace it.

---

## 2. Locked Production Requirements

All Hero Service posters inherit these technical requirements:

| Item | Standard |
|---|---|
| Canvas | **1080 × 1350 px** |
| Aspect ratio | **4:5** |
| Safe margin | **60 px on all sides** |
| Safe-area size | **960 × 1230 px** |
| Finalization | **Canva** |
| Primary CTA | **WhatsApp** |
| Current WhatsApp | **0897-7706-838** |
| Brand Instagram | **@bakuedit.tugas** |

Do not rely on AI output for exact dimensions. Exact canvas size must be enforced during production/export.

All important content must remain inside the safe area.

---

## 3. Reference Poster

The approved Hero Service #1 poster is the primary visual reference for the series.

Reference characteristics:
- cream/off-white base;
- navy and gold primary accents;
- strong editorial hierarchy;
- large problem-led headline;
- service-specific hero visual;
- rounded content blocks;
- three repeated service/support cards;
- value proposition strip;
- prominent WhatsApp CTA;
- concise trust/support footer.

The reference poster should be used to maintain visual continuity, not copied literally.

---

## 4. Core Visual Architecture

The default Hero Service composition follows this order:

1. **Header / Logo Area**
2. **Hero Service Label**
3. **Problem-led Headline**
4. **Service Proposition / Supporting Copy**
5. **Hero Visual**
6. **Three Service/Benefit Cards**
7. **Value Proposition Strip**
8. **WhatsApp CTA**
9. **Trust / Support Footer**

Sections may be compressed or adapted when content requires it, but the overall hierarchy should remain recognizable.

Do not add sections merely to fill empty space.

---

## 5. Header and Logo Area

The header reserves a dedicated area for the BAKU Edit Tugas logo.

Rules:
- The logo is an approved brand asset.
- AI must not redraw, regenerate, recolor, or reinterpret the logo.
- During AI generation, leave a clean logo placeholder/space.
- Insert the approved logo manually during Canva finalization.
- Preserve the logo's original proportions.
- Do not stretch or distort the logo.

The logo area should align with the main left-side content column and maintain clear separation from the Hero Service badge.

---

## 6. Hero Service Badge

Each poster carries a consistent service-series label:

> **HERO SERVICE #X**

Recommended treatment:
- navy rounded rectangle;
- white primary text;
- gold service number;
- optional simple gold icon such as a star;
- positioned in the upper-right area within the safe margin.

The badge should remain visually consistent across the series. Only the service number and, where necessary, the icon may change.

---

## 7. Headline System

Hero Service headlines are problem-led and should communicate the viewer's situation immediately.

Default structure:

**Problem → Service → Benefit**

Examples of approved series logic:
- Hero #1: a problem related to thesis review;
- Hero #2: a problem related to proofreading;
- subsequent heroes: a problem specific to the service being promoted.

Rules:
- Headline must be the strongest typographic element.
- Use concise wording.
- Avoid long explanatory headlines.
- Maintain strong contrast between navy and gold.
- Keep the headline within the safe area.

The headline must not become identical across Hero Service posters; each poster needs a distinct service proposition.

---

## 8. Hero Visual

The hero visual should immediately communicate the service being promoted.

For academic editing services, appropriate visual language includes:
- academic documents;
- thesis pages;
- laptops/tablets;
- highlighted or reviewed text;
- notebooks and pens;
- reference books;
- clean desk/workspace scenes.

Avoid unrelated stock imagery or visuals that imply unsupported claims.

The visual should have enough negative space and should not compete with the main headline.

---

## 9. Information / Card System

The reference application uses three repeated cards.

Each card should maintain:
- comparable width;
- comparable internal padding;
- consistent icon placement;
- consistent number/title hierarchy where numbering is used;
- aligned baselines;
- comparable description spacing;
- a consistent accent treatment.

### Card content

Use the three cards to explain the most important components, benefits, or sub-services of the Hero Service.

Do not simply repeat the service name three times.

### Shape rule

All major cards use **rounded rectangles** with a consistent radius language.

Avoid brush-stroke panels, arbitrary geometric blocks, or inconsistent shapes unless a separate creative direction is explicitly approved.

---

## 10. Value Proposition Strip

A concise value proposition should connect the service details to the practical outcome for the customer.

Recommended characteristics:
- rounded rectangle container;
- cream/light background or approved brand treatment;
- navy and gold typography;
- one short supporting message;
- optional reinforcing icon.

Keep the message outcome-oriented without making unsupported guarantees.

Avoid exaggerated academic or outcome claims such as guaranteed grades, guaranteed graduation, or guaranteed acceptance.

---

## 11. WhatsApp CTA

The CTA is one of the strongest elements near the bottom of the poster.

Default structure:

> **[CTA headline]** | **[WhatsApp icon] 0897-7706-838**

Rules:
- Make the CTA visually obvious.
- Keep the number highly legible.
- The WhatsApp icon may sit immediately to the left of the number.
- Do not duplicate the WhatsApp icon elsewhere in the same poster without a clear functional reason.
- Keep the CTA inside the 60 px safe margin.

---

## 12. Trust / Support Footer

The bottom support area may use the established four-item structure from Hero #1:

- Rapi & Jelas
- Respons Cepat & Mudah
- Privasi Terjaga
- Kualitas Terjamin

These are positioning/reassurance statements and must not become unsupported guarantees.

Keep four-column items visually balanced and aligned.

The Instagram handle does not need to appear in the bottom footer unless explicitly required by the poster brief.

---

## 13. Grid, Spacing, and Alignment

Use a consistent grid across the entire series.

Priority order:

1. Safe margin compliance
2. Major section alignment
3. Repeated card alignment
4. Baseline consistency
5. Vertical rhythm
6. Micro-spacing
7. Decorative refinement

When repeated cards exist, use the same column widths and internal structure.

Maintain deliberate spacing between:
- header and headline;
- headline and hero visual/proposition;
- hero area and information cards;
- cards and value proposition;
- value proposition and CTA;
- CTA and trust footer.

Do not compress sections simply to fit more content.

Micro-alignment takes precedence over additional decoration.

---

## 14. Color and Typography

Use the established BAKU Edit Tugas visual language:

**Primary palette**
- Navy / deep blue
- Gold / yellow
- Cream / off-white
- White

Typography must maintain a clear hierarchy between:
- headline;
- supporting statement;
- service/card titles;
- body copy;
- CTA.

Avoid unnecessary font variation.

---

## 15. Iconography

Icons should use a coherent visual family.

Maintain consistency in:
- stroke/weight;
- complexity;
- scale;
- color treatment;
- circular or rounded containers where applicable.

Icons should reinforce meaning rather than fill empty space.

---

## 16. Hero Service Series Rules

Current approved service sequence:

1. **Hero Service #1 — Review Skripsi**
2. **Hero Service #2 — Proofreading**
3. **Hero Service #3 — Editing Grammar**
4. **Hero Service #4 — Formatting**
5. **Hero Service #5 — Sitasi & Referensi**
6. **Hero Service #6 — Konsultasi Tulis**

Each Hero Service must:
- use the same core visual system;
- promote only the approved service;
- have a distinct problem/message;
- use a service-relevant hero visual;
- avoid duplicating another Hero Service's proposition.

The series should feel like variations of one brand system, not six unrelated poster designs.

---

## 17. Production Workflow

Use the following workflow for every Hero Service:

**Content Brief → Visual Direction → Generation → Visual QC → Exact 1080×1350 Production → Canva Finalization → Final QC → Export**

### AI generation

AI may create the hero visual and supporting visual treatment.

AI must not be relied upon for:
- exact logo rendering;
- exact copy accuracy;
- exact pixel dimensions;
- final typography;
- precision spacing/alignment.

### Canva

Canva is the finalization layer for:
- logo placement;
- final typography;
- alignment;
- spacing;
- CTA placement;
- final copy accuracy;
- brand-asset fidelity.

---

## 18. Final QC

Before final approval, verify:

### Brand
- Looks unmistakably like BAKU Edit Tugas.
- Matches the established Hero Service visual language.
- Uses the approved logo asset correctly.

### Content
- Service name matches the service catalog.
- Problem and benefit are immediately understandable.
- No unsupported claims.
- CTA is clear.

### Visual
- Hierarchy is obvious.
- Major blocks use rounded rectangles.
- Cards align consistently.
- Typography is readable.
- Spacing is balanced.
- Hero visual supports the service.

### Technical
- **1080 × 1350 px**
- **4:5**
- **60 px safe margin**
- No non-uniform stretching.

### Conversion
- The viewer understands the service.
- The viewer understands the practical benefit.
- The viewer knows the next action.

Findings must be classified as:
- **Critical issue** — must be fixed before final
- **Improvement** — should be fixed
- **Preference** — optional
