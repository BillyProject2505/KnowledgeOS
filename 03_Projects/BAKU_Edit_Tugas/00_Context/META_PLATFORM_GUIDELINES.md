# BAKU Edit Tugas — Meta Platform Guidelines

**Status:** WORKING — evidence-backed reference  
**Scope:** Instagram and Facebook content production for BAKU Edit Tugas  
**Last Verified:** 2026-08-30

## 1. Purpose

This document is the project-level reference for using Meta guidance when producing BAKU Edit Tugas content for Instagram and Facebook.

It is not a description of Meta's internal ranking algorithm and must not be used to make guarantees about reach, engagement, recommendation, or virality.

## 2. Source Hierarchy

Use this hierarchy when deciding whether a claim is authoritative:

1. Meta official policy and Recommendation Guidelines — canonical policy evidence.
2. Meta official Creator / Business guidance — canonical best-practice evidence within its stated scope.
3. AI at Meta documentation — canonical evidence for Meta AI capabilities and Meta's published AI-assisted content guidance.
4. Meta Help Center / Account Status guidance — authoritative operational guidance.
5. Meta Newsroom / Transparency Center — authoritative announcements and enforcement explanations.
6. Third-party expert analysis — supporting evidence only.
7. Creator/community observations — observations or hypotheses, not policy.

A lower-level source must not override a higher-level official source.

## 3. Evidence Labels

Use these labels for statements in this document and in production decisions:

- **OFFICIAL_POLICY** — directly supported by Meta policy/documentation.
- **OFFICIAL_GUIDANCE** — official Meta best practice or educational guidance.
- **META_AI_CAPABILITY** — capability described by AI at Meta or Meta AI materials.
- **OBSERVATION** — external observed pattern; not an official rule.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — unverified interpretation.
- **UNKNOWN** — not established by available evidence.

## 4. Meta AI — Relevant Capabilities

AI at Meta documents Meta AI as useful for generating and refining social-media captions and for suggesting hashtags from prompts. Meta's Instagram caption guidance says users can provide format, objective, audience, and brand voice to generate platform-appropriate caption options. It also describes help with hooks, calls to action, emojis, and niche hashtags. citeturn737999search2

AI at Meta also publishes guidance for using AI to create hashtags, including prompts that ask for relevant hashtags and different competition tiers. citeturn737999search3

### BAKU Operating Rule

Meta AI may be used as a **drafting and review assistant**, not as the authority for policy or ranking guarantees.

Recommended workflow:

`Brief → Draft → Meta AI Assistance → Human/ChatGPT Editorial Review → Policy/Recommendation Check → Finalize → Publish → Observe`

## 5. Caption Guidance

### Officially supported principles

- Captions should add context and meaning to the visual/content. citeturn737999search1turn737999search2
- Meta AI can generate audience-aware, format-aware caption drafts when the prompt contains clear objectives, audience, format, and brand voice. citeturn737999search2
- Meta's published guidance supports using captions to add context, encourage appropriate action, and improve discoverability when relevant. citeturn737999search1turn737999search2
- Meta has explicitly identified long, distracting captions and unrelated captions as spammy behavior on Facebook that can reduce distribution and affect monetization. citeturn732892search0

### BAKU rules

- Write naturally and keep copy relevant to the actual content.
- Do not use keyword stuffing.
- Do not add unrelated text for distribution purposes.
- Use hooks and CTAs only when they genuinely support the content objective.
- Do not promise reach or engagement because of a caption format.

## 6. Hashtag Guidance

AI at Meta documents Meta AI as capable of suggesting hashtags based on a prompt and provides examples using targeted hashtags and competition tiers. citeturn737999search3

Meta's published Instagram guidance has also described using fewer, more targeted hashtags as preferable to large amounts of hashtags. Because platform rollout and product limits can change by account and time, exact technical limits must be treated as **current product behavior to verify**, not as a timeless algorithm rule.

### BAKU rules

- Prefer relevant, targeted hashtags over generic or unrelated hashtags.
- Do not use hashtags merely to manufacture reach signals.
- Do not claim a fixed hashtag count guarantees reach or recommendation.
- When preparing a post, verify the currently accepted technical hashtag limit in the live Instagram product/account rather than relying on an old limit.
- Hashtag recommendations from Meta AI are suggestions, not ranking guarantees.

### Important correction

Do **not** store `5 hashtags` as a permanent BAKU algorithm rule. The number may reflect current Instagram product guidance or rollout behavior at a given time, but it is not evidence of a universal ranking formula.

## 7. Search / Keywords

AI at Meta's Instagram caption guidance states that captions can contribute context and discoverability and that relevant hashtags and keywords can help people find content. citeturn737999search2

### BAKU rules

- Use keywords naturally and only when they describe the actual content.
- Do not create keyword-heavy captions that reduce readability.
- There is no canonical BAKU rule for a specific keyword position, density, or count.
- Do not describe keywords or hashtags as a "ranking hack."

## 8. Recommendation Eligibility vs Platform Permission

BAKU must distinguish three concepts:

### Allowed on the platform

Content that does not violate applicable platform policies.

### Eligible for recommendation

Content that meets Meta's recommendation requirements and may therefore be surfaced to people who do not already follow the account.

### Distribution / ranking

The systems that determine what content is shown to whom and where. Meta does not publish a complete deterministic formula that can be used to guarantee outcomes.

For production, use official Recommendation Guidelines and Account Status / related platform feedback as the operational reference.

## 9. Spammy and Manipulative Practices

Meta has explicitly said that on Facebook, accounts that use tactics such as unrelated captions, excessively hashtagged captions, spam networks, or coordinated fake engagement can face reduced reach, reduced visibility, and/or monetization consequences. citeturn732892search0

### BAKU DON'T

- Engagement bait designed primarily to manufacture interaction.
- Fake or coordinated engagement.
- Irrelevant captions.
- Distracting or spammy caption construction.
- Excessive or irrelevant hashtags.
- Repetitive spammy publishing patterns.
- Impersonation.
- Fabricated social proof.
- Misleading service claims.
- Content intentionally designed to game distribution.

These should be classified according to the actual policy or recommendation guidance that applies; do not label every poor practice a policy violation.

## 10. Originality / Reused Content

Meta has stated that it is investing in helping creators of original content succeed and that unoriginal content can be excluded from recommendation contexts. citeturn732892search0

### BAKU rule

Prefer original, brand-relevant content. If third-party material is used, ensure the use complies with applicable rights and platform rules and does not rely on superficial repackaging as a substitute for original value.

## 11. AI-Generated Content

Meta AI's own guidance establishes AI as a content-production assistant, but the use of Meta AI does not itself constitute policy approval or guarantee recommendation eligibility. Meta AI output must still be reviewed for accuracy, relevance, brand fit, originality, and applicable platform rules. citeturn737999search4turn737999search2

Do not state that AI-generated content is automatically penalized. That is **UNKNOWN** unless supported by a current official Meta source.

## 12. BAKU Content Safety / Claim Rules

The following are project-level rules:

- No fabricated testimonials or social proof.
- No guaranteed-result claims such as guaranteed graduation or guaranteed grades.
- No unsupported performance claims such as "2× more reach".
- No unsupported absolute privacy/security claims such as `100% Private`.
- All service claims must be factually supportable by BAKU's actual offering.

These are BAKU editorial controls and must not be represented as direct quotations of Meta policy unless a specific Meta source supports them.

## 14. Unknown / Not Officially Established

Unless a current official source establishes otherwise, treat these as unknown or non-canonical:

- Exact weights used by ranking systems.
- A guaranteed best posting time.
- A guaranteed reach multiplier from any hashtag or caption formula.
- A fixed number of hashtags that guarantees recommendation.
- A guaranteed ranking benefit from a particular CTA format.
- A secret list of words that automatically suppresses reach.

## 15. Pre-Publication Checklist

### Content

- [ ] Caption is relevant to the actual content.
- [ ] Claim is supportable.
- [ ] No fabricated proof or statistics.
- [ ] No manipulative engagement bait.
- [ ] No unnecessary keyword stuffing.

### Hashtags

- [ ] Every hashtag is relevant.
- [ ] Hashtags are targeted rather than padded for volume.
- [ ] Current platform hashtag limits have been checked when relevant.
- [ ] No claim that hashtag quantity guarantees performance.

### Platform

- [ ] Recommendation eligibility guidance has been considered where relevant.
- [ ] Current Account Status / platform feedback is checked when diagnosing distribution issues.
- [ ] AI-generated content has received human/editorial review.

When Meta changes a policy, product limit, recommendation rule, or creator guidance:

3. Record the change in `00_Context/CHANGELOG.md`.
4. Update relevant production decisions if the change affects BAKU.
