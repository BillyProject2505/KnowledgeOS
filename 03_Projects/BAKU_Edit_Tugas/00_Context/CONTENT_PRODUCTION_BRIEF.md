# BAKU Edit Tugas — Content Production Brief

**Status:** WORKING — owner inputs required for BAKU-specific fields marked `NEEDS OWNER`.

## Purpose

Provide the minimum project context that should be available before producing captions, hashtags, Stories, Reels, or Facebook posts.

This document is an **input contract**, not a substitute for `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, or `META_PLATFORM_GUIDELINES.md`.

## Source Hierarchy

Use the following authority order when producing content:

1. BAKU-specific approved project facts and decisions.
2. Current approved content and brand assets.
3. Current verified Meta platform guidance.
4. Meta AI assistance.
5. Editorial judgment and experimentation, clearly labeled as such.

Project-specific truth always overrides generic platform suggestions.

## Meta AI Role

Meta AI may assist with:

- ideation;
- caption drafting;
- caption rewriting;
- alternative versions;
- hook and CTA suggestions;
- hashtag suggestions;
- content refinement;
- content review;
- platform-aware adaptation;
- performance insight assistance where the current Meta AI product supports it.

Meta AI is **not** the authority for:

- BAKU facts or service claims;
- final brand positioning;
- platform policy interpretation;
- recommendation eligibility decisions;
- guaranteed reach, engagement, or virality;
- final publication approval.

## Review Requirements

Before final publication:

1. Verify BAKU-specific claims against approved project facts.
2. Review wording for clarity, relevance, and brand fit.
3. Check applicable Meta policy/recommendation guidance.
4. Check current platform-specific product constraints when relevant.
5. Confirm that canonical brand assets are used.
6. Confirm CTA/contact details.
7. Record new approved project decisions in `DECISIONS.md` before treating them as canonical.

## Evidence Handling

Do not treat the following as equivalent:

- `OFFICIAL_POLICY`
- `OFFICIAL_GUIDANCE`
- `META_AI_CAPABILITY`
- `OBSERVATION`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `UNKNOWN`

When a production recommendation depends on evidence, preserve its evidence type and avoid overstating certainty.

## Approval Boundary

Owner/project decisions control BAKU-specific facts, services, claims, audience, brand voice, visual identity, and final publication decisions.

Meta guidance informs platform-aware production but does not override project-specific truth.

Meta AI assists production but does not create canonical BAKU policy merely by generating a recommendation.
