## D-007 — FAQ Highlight Format

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** APPROVED

The FAQ Highlight will be produced as static Instagram Stories in 9:16 format at a target size of 1080 × 1920 px. The output is static imagery; video and animation are not part of this workstream.

**Reason:** The Highlight is being designed as a persistent, profile-based FAQ and decision-support asset rather than as a daily distribution-focused content format.

---

## D-008 — Four-Story FAQ Structure

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** APPROVED

The FAQ Highlight consists of four Stories with these purposes:

1. Positioning — distinguish BAKU from a "joki" perception.
2. Speed — communicate an expected 1–3 day timeframe, qualified by length and level of work.
3. Privacy — communicate that files are not distributed and can be requested for deletion after completion.
4. Accessibility + Conversion — communicate that service is available outside Manado, is 100% online, and provide the main inquiry CTA.

## D-012 — Conservative Safe-Area Principle

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** APPROVED

Important Story elements should use a conservative safe area informed by Meta guidance: approximately 14% from the top, 20% from the bottom, and 6% from the left/right. The previously discussed 1080 × 1420 px figure is not an official Instagram safe-zone specification.

**Reason:** Important copy, logo, and CTA elements should avoid likely overlap with Instagram UI.

---

## D-013 — Copy Principle

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** APPROVED

Story copy should be as short as possible without removing necessary context. The series uses one question, one direct answer, supporting copy only when needed, and one CTA where appropriate. There is no fixed official 18–22 word limit.

---

## D-014 — Evidence and Claim Boundaries

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** APPROVED

The production should not rely on unsupported platform-performance claims, fabricated statistics, fabricated social proof, or unsupported absolute security/privacy claims. In particular, do not use a claim that a CTA pill performs "2× better" without evidence.

The following are accepted as strategic principles for this workstream: immediate comprehension is important for Story openings; Highlights can function as persistent profile-based decision-support assets; and safe-area guidance should inform layout.

---

## D-016 — Production Sequence

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Status:** APPROVED

The production sequence is:

`Concept → Master Frame → Generation → Visual QC → Canva → Final QC → Export`

Within the current workstream, the immediate gate is to design and lock the Master Story Frame first. After Master Frame approval, Story 1 is the first visual prototype and is used to validate the system before replicating it to Stories 2–4.

**Reason:** Validate the reusable system before spending production effort on all four Stories.
